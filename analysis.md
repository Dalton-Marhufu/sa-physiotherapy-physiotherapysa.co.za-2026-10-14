## Top 3 Issues
- The homepage is a flat wall of text from an ageing WordPress/Divi theme with no hero image, weak visual hierarchy and no clear structure for a first-time visitor.
- There is no obvious call-to-action anywhere — no booking button, no click-to-call prominence — so a motivated patient has no clear next step.
- Contact details for four separate practices are buried in plain text with no mapping, hours clarity or dedicated location cards, which hurts both trust and mobile usability.

## Top 3 Improvements
- Lead with a strong hero using their own photography, a clear headline and prominent click-to-call / booking actions above the fold.
- Rebuild the services as a visual grid of the conditions they actually treat (back and neck pain, sports injuries, post-surgical, paediatrics and more), each with a real image and short description.
- Present the four practices (Alberton, Medimix, Mulbarton, Oakdene) as clean, scannable cards with hours, phone and email, plus a team section to reinforce credibility.
