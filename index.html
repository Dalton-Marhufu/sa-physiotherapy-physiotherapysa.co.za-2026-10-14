What we treat

A varied range of physiotherapy treatments

Physiotherapists are first-line practitioners — you don't need a referral from a doctor. We assess, diagnose and decide on the right treatment, referring you on only when it's necessary.

Back & Neck Pain

Assessment and hands-on treatment to relieve pain and restore movement in the spine.

Sports Injuries

Rehabilitation and return-to-sport programmes for acute and overuse injuries.

Post-Surgical Conditions

Structured recovery after orthopaedic and general surgery to build strength and function.

Headaches

Treatment of tension and cervicogenic headaches linked to the neck and posture.

Postural-Related Problems

Correcting the desk-bound aches and imbalances that creep into daily life.

Strokes

Neurological rehabilitation to help patients regain independence and daily function.

Paediatric Conditions

Gentle, specialist care for children's movement, development and respiratory needs.

Bronchitis & Bronchopneumonia

Chest physiotherapy to clear secretions and ease breathing during respiratory illness.

Our practice

An impeccable reputation, built one patient at a time

We've provided physiotherapy to the community in the south of Johannesburg since 1976, and in more recent years we've extended our service into the East Rand. Over the years we've earned the trust of both our medical colleagues and the people we treat.

Our goal is simple: help patients regain their function so they can return to work, sport and everyday life. We keep abreast of new evidence-based techniques and spend quality time with every patient — because we know physiotherapy works.

  • Personal service with dedicated one-on-one sessions
  • A team approach alongside your doctor and specialists
  • Fees charged within prescribed medical scheme rates
